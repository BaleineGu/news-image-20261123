# Monday 23 November 2026: 40 stories, 6 continents, one front page and a map

A one-image newspaper front page for Monday 23 November 2026, built for
the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123)
($100, top 50 paid, deadline 2026-11-25T00:00:00Z).

## What's on the page

- **Three illustrated leads:** Trump threatens fresh strikes on Iran as
  tankers burn in Hormuz; COP31 fails to agree a climate deal in
  Antalya; Zelenskyy vows to drop the Kerch bridge again if Russia
  rebuilds it.
- **Six smaller illustrated stories:** GTA VI's record $3.79bn launch,
  the Democrats' historic House majority, gold sliding on Fed hike
  fears, Trump's warning over China's nuclear build-up, drones hitting
  a UN building in Khartoum, and India–Nepal leader talks.
- **By the numbers:** $3.79bn GTA VI day-one sales · 258+ Democratic
  House seats · gold −9% in a week · 25 tankers hit near Hormuz in a
  week · India's record 720 in Wellington.
- **A dotted world map** with numbered dots for the nine illustrated
  stories, a region table, and a numbered list taking the day to
  **40 stories** from Asia-Pacific, Europe, North America, the Middle
  East, Africa and South America.
- **Sources footer:** Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, BBC, CNN, FT, IMD, NASA,
  UN, UEFA, ESPNcricinfo. Disputed claims are attributed.

Every story was checked against reporting dated 23 November 2026.
Continuing stories (GTA VI's launch, the Wellington Test, the Peru
quake) are framed by their 23 November development: the three-day
sales milestone, the final day of the Test, and the damage assessment
still under way.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the finished
`entry.png` (1024×1024 JPEG, under 1 MiB) to the grader's output
folder (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output`) byte for byte and verifies
the copy by size and SHA-256, so two runs write identical bytes. No
network, no third-party packages.
